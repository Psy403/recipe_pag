Beef Shawarma

Beef Shawarma Wraps bring rich, warm Middle Eastern flavors straight to your lunch table.
Tender, lean beef strips are seasoned with aromatic spices, seared until juicy, and wrapped in warm pita bread with crunchy onions and tomatoes.
Topped with a creamy garlic or tahini sauce, this wrap offers a perfect balance of savory, smoky, and fresh flavors.






Ingredients

  • 150g lean beef, cut into thin strips
  • 1 pita bread or flatbread wrap
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin, paprika, and garlic powder
  • 1/2 cup sliced onions and tomatoes
  • 1 tablespoon tahini or garlic sauce
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Cut the lean beef into very thin, bite-sized strips.
  2. Place the beef strips in a mixing bowl.
  3. Add the cumin, paprika, garlic powder, salt, and olive oil to the beef.
  4. Mix well with your hands and let the beef marinate for 10 minutes to absorb the flavors.
  5. While the beef marinates, thinly slice your onions and tomatoes.
  6. Heat a frying pan over medium-high heat.
  7. Add the marinated beef to the hot pan and cook for 6 to 8 minutes until fully cooked and slightly crispy.
  8. Warm the pita bread or flatbread on a pan for about 30 seconds.
  9. Spread a generous tablespoon of garlic sauce or tahini over the warm bread.
  10. Layer the cooked beef, sliced onions, and tomatoes in the center, fold the sides inward, roll it up tightly, and enjoy.
