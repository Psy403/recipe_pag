Chicken Salad

Chicken salad is a light, protein-packed lunch that keeps you energized throughout the afternoon without making you feel bloated.
It’s incredibly versatile, combining tender chicken breast with crisp, fresh vegetables and a simple, tangy dressing.
Perfect for quick meal prep, it is a refreshing and clean way to fuel your body.






Ingredients

  • 1 cooked chicken breast, shredded or diced
  • 1 cup chopped lettuce or spinach
  • 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/2 cucumber, sliced
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Toss the chopped lettuce, cherry tomatoes, and cucumber slices together in a large bowl.
  2. Add the shredded or diced chicken breast to the bowl of vegetables.
  3. Whisk the olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and pepper in a small cup, then drizzle it over the salad.
  4. Mix everything gently until well combined and serve immediately.
