Golden Tumeric Milk

Golden Turmeric Milk, also known as Haldi Doodh, is an ancient, deeply healing Ayurvedic drink celebrated for its powerful health benefits.
This warm, soothing beverage combines the anti-inflammatory properties of turmeric with warming spices like black pepper, cinnamon, and ginger.
It is the ultimate comforting bedtime drink that boosts immunity, aids sleep, and relaxes the body after a long day.







Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups milk (dairy or plant-based like almond or coconut milk)
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground turmeric
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 teaspoon grated fresh ginger or ginger powder
  • A pinch of freshly ground black pepper (essential for absorbing turmeric)
  • 1 teaspoon honey or maple syrup
  • 1/2 teaspoon coconut oil or ghee (optional, helps absorption)

Instructions

  1. Pour the milk into a small saucepan and place it over medium-low heat.
  2. Whisk in the turmeric, cinnamon, ginger, black pepper, and optional coconut oil until the spices are well combined.
  3. Bring the mixture to a very gentle simmer, watching closely so that the milk does not boil over.
  4. Reduce the heat to low and let it simmer gently for 5 to 7 minutes to allow the spices to fully infuse.
  5. Turn off the heat and strain the warm milk through a fine sieve into a mug to remove any bits of ginger.
  6. Stir in the honey or maple syrup to sweeten, dust with an extra pinch of cinnamon, and enjoy warm.
