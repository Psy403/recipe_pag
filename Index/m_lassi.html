Mango Lassi

Mango Lassi is a luxurious, creamy, and traditional South Asian beverage that blends the tropical sweetness of ripe mangoes with the tanginess of yogurt.
Perfectly spiced with a hint of cardamom, this velvet-smooth drink is both satisfying and incredibly cooling.
It serves as a delicious dessert-like beverage that pairs beautifully with spicy dishes or stands alone as a rich afternoon treat.






Ingredients

  • 1 cup ripe mango pulp (fresh or canned)
  • 1 cup fresh plain yogurt (thick curd)
  • 1/2 cup chilled milk or water
  • 2 tablespoons sugar or honey (adjust to taste)
  • 1/4 teaspoon cardamom powder
  • A few saffron strands (optional)
  • Crushed ice
  • Chopped pistachios or almonds (for garnish)

Instructions

  1. Peel the ripe mango, chop the flesh into small pieces, and place them directly into a blender.
  2. Add the plain yogurt, chilled milk, sugar, cardamom powder, and optional saffron to the blender.
  3. Throw in a small handful of crushed ice to make the drink instantly cold.
  4. Blend on high speed for 30 to 45 seconds until the mixture is completely thick, smooth, and creamy.
  5. If the lassi is too thick, stir in a splash of extra milk and blend for another 5 seconds.
  6. Pour into tall glasses, garnish with chopped pistachios or almonds, and serve immediately.
