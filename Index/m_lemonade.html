Mint Lemonade

Mint Lemonade is an incredibly refreshing and hydrating drink that perfectly balances tangy lemons with cool, crisp mint.
It is the ultimate thirst-quencher for hot summer days, helping to instantly lower body temperature and aid digestion.
Crisp, zesty, and naturally energizing, this vibrant green cooler is a crowd-pleaser that takes only minutes to whip up.






Ingredients

  • 1 cup fresh mint leaves
  • 2 large lemons (juiced)
  • 3 tablespoons sugar or honey
  • 3 cups chilled water
  • 1/2 teaspoon black salt or regular salt
  • Ice cubes
  • Lemon slices and mint sprigs (for garnish)

Instructions

  1. Wash the fresh mint leaves thoroughly and squeeze the juice from the lemons, removing any seeds.
  2. Add the mint leaves, lemon juice, sugar (or honey), salt, and one cup of chilled water into a blender.
  3. Blend on high speed until the mint is completely pulverized and the liquid turns a vibrant green.
  4. Strain the mixture through a fine-mesh sieve into a pitcher to remove any leftover pulp.
  5. Stir in the remaining two cups of chilled water and adjust the sweetness or tartness to your liking.
  6. Pour the lemonade into glasses filled with ice cubes, garnish with lemon slices and mint sprigs, and serve.
