Paneer Roll

A Paneer Roll is a delicious, protein-rich vegetarian lunch that wraps spiced cottage cheese and crunchy veggies
into one convenient bite. Soft paneer chunks are sautéed to perfection with aromatic spices and rolled inside a warm
whole-wheat flatbread. It is a satisfying, flavorful, and mess-free meal that is perfect for busy workdays.






Ingredients

  • 100g paneer (cottage cheese), cut into small cubes
  • 1 whole-wheat roti or tortilla wrap
  • 1/2 cup sliced onions and bell peppers
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric and chili powder
  • 1 tablespoon mint chutney or Greek yogurt
  • 1 teaspoon oil
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Sauté the paneer cubes in a pan with oil, turmeric, chili powder, and salt until they turn light golden.
  2. Toss the sliced onions and bell peppers into the same pan and cook for 2 minutes so they stay slightly crunchy.
  3. Warm the roti or wrap, spread the mint chutney or yogurt on it, and place the paneer-veggie mix in the center.
  4. Roll the wrap tightly from one side to the other, wrap the bottom in foil or paper, and enjoy.
