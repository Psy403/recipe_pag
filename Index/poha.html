Poha

Poha is a popular Indian breakfast and snack dish made from flattened rice (also known as beaten or parched rice),
which is parboiled, dried, and rolled into thin flakes. The word refers to both the ingredient and the dish, with
regional variations like Kanda Poha (onion poha), Batata Poha (potato poha), and Indori Poha being staples in
Western and Central India.






Ingredients

  • 1 cup poha (flattened rice),
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 1 small potato, diced
  • Green peas or your favorite veggies
  • 1 green chili, chopped
  • Mustard seeds and turmeric powder
  • Oil and salt
  • Lemon juice
  • Roasted peanuts and fresh coriander (optional)

Instructions

  1. Put the poha in a strainer, rinse it gently under water to soften it, and set it aside.
  2. Heat oil in a pan, splutter the mustard seeds, then sauté the onion, chili, potato, and peas until they are soft.
  3. Stir in a pinch of turmeric and salt to taste.
  4. Add the rinsed poha, mix everything gently so the grains don't break, and cook on low heat for 2 to 3 minutes.
  5. Squeeze fresh lemon juice on top, garnish with coriander and peanuts, and serve hot.
