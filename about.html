About Us

Who we are

We has helped take the stress out of feeding your family. Whether you’re cooking for a crew of two or the whole Brady bunch, we offer trusted, family-friendly recipes and practical cooking advice to make mealtimes easier.

All our recipes are professionally developed, rigorously tested, and designed to work in real home kitchens so they’ll taste just as delicious at your table as they do at ours. We also help ease dinnertime anxiety with simple yet effective cooking tips, recommendations for grocery and kitchen products (the same ones that we use ourselves), and fresh ideas to inspire your cooking.

Simply our team of editors, recipe developers and testers, writers, and photographers are the primary cooks and grocery shoppers for our families, just like our readers. That means we understand your needs—because they’re our needs too. Whether it’s our series of one-ingredient upgrades passed down through generations, smart meal prep tips for the next holiday, or a simple Coca-Cola peanut snack that reminds us of childhood summers, our recipes and stories reflect the many kitchens, cultures, and traditions of our families.

Our priority is to share recipes you’ll want to make again and again. That’s why more than 8 million home cooks turn to us each month for our reliable, accessible, and family-friendly food content. Creating “kitchen wins” is what we’re all about.
