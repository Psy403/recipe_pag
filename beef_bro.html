Beef and Broccoli Stir-Fry

Beef and Broccoli Stir-Fry is a classic, healthy take on a beloved restaurant favorite that you can easily whip up at home
It features tender, juicy strips of beef and crisp-tender green broccoli florets tossed in a savory, garlic-infused soy sauce glaze
High in protein and low in carbs, this quick meal delivers a perfect balance of rich, savory flavor and satisfying textures.

Ingredients

  • 200g chicken breast or thighs, sliced or cubed
  • 150g egg or wheat noodles
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 green chilies, sliced
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1/2 cup carrots, julienned
  • 1/2 cup bell peppers, sliced
  • 1 teaspoon cumin powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon garam masala
  • 3 cups chicken broth
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Fresh coriander and lemon wedges for garnish

Instructions

  1. Cook the noodles in boiling water according to the package instructions until they are soft.
  2. Drain the noodles, rinse them with cold water, toss with a few drops of oil to prevent sticking, and set aside.
  3. Heat one tablespoon of oil in a deep pot over medium heat.
  4. Add the chopped onions and sliced green chilies, sautéing until the onions turn translucent.
  5. Stir in the ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1 minute until the raw smell goes away.
  6. Add the chopped tomatoes, cumin, turmeric, garam masala, salt, and pepper, cooking until the tomatoes soften.
  7. Add the chicken pieces to the pot and sauté them for 4 to 5 minutes until they start to brown.
  8. Pour in the chicken broth and bring the entire mixture to a gentle boil.
  9. Lower the heat, cover the pot with a lid, and let it simmer for 10 minutes until the chicken is fully cooked.
  10. Carefully remove the chicken from the pot, shred it into bite-sized pieces with two forks, and slide it back into the broth.
  11. Add the sliced carrots and bell peppers to the simmering soup.
  12. Stir in the soy sauce and let the vegetables cook for 3 to 4 minutes so they stay slightly crunchy.
  13. Place a portion of the cooked noodles into your serving bowls.
  14. Ladle the hot chicken and vegetable broth generously over the noodles.
  15. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve immediately with a squeeze of fresh lemon juice.
