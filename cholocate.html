Chocolate Chip Cookie

This is the best chocolate chip cookies recipe ever! No funny ingredients, no chilling time, etc. Just a simple, straightforward, amazingly delicious, doughy yet still fully cooked, chocolate chip cookie that turns out perfectly every single time!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of unsalted butter, softened
  • 1 cup of sugar
  • 1 cup of borwn sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 3 cup all purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 cups chocolate chip

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degree Fahrenheit (175 degree Celsius).
  2. In a large bowl. cream together butter and sugars until smooth,
  3. Beal in eggs and vanilla.
  4. Gradually add dry ingredients to wet ingredients and mix well.
  5. Stir in chocolate chips,
  6. Bake for 10-12 minutes or until edges are golden brown.
  7. Let cool before serving.
