Korean Beef Skillet

The Korean Beef Skillet is a fast, flavor-packed meal that brings the sweet, savory, and slightly spicy elements of Korean BBQ into an easy, one-pan lunch.
It combines lean ground beef sautéed with aromatic garlic, ginger, and green onions, all coated in a delicious sesame-soy glaze.
It is an incredibly versatile recipe that is perfect for meal prep, pairing beautifully with rice or served inside crisp lettuce wraps.

Ingredients

  • 250g lean ground beef
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon fresh ginger, grated
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon brown sugar or honey
  • 1 teaspoon sesame oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes
  • 3 green onions (scallions), chopped
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • Sesame seeds for garnish
  • Sliced mushrooms or bell peppers (optional)

Instructions

  1. In a small bowl, mix together the soy sauce, brown sugar, sesame oil, and red pepper flakes until the sugar dissolves.
  2. Heat one tablespoon of cooking oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  3. Add the ground beef to the hot skillet.
  4. Cook the beef for 5 to 6 minutes, using a wooden spoon to break it up into small crumbles as it browns.
  5. If you are using optional veggies like mushrooms or peppers, add them to the pan now.
  6. Continue to cook until the beef is fully browned and no pink parts remain.
  7. Carefully drain any excess fat or liquid from the skillet to keep the dish light.
  8. Push the beef to one side of the skillet to create a little open space.
  9. Add the minced garlic and grated ginger to the empty space, sautéing them for 1 minute until fragrant.
  10. Stir the garlic and ginger back into the beef to combine the flavors.
  11. Pour the prepared soy sauce mixture evenly over the beef.
  12. Stir everything together thoroughly so the meat absorbs the sauce.
  13. Let the mixture simmer for 2 to 3 minutes on low heat until the sauce reduces and glazes the beef.
  14. Fold in most of the chopped green onions, saving a small handful for the final touch.
  15. Turn off the heat, sprinkle the remaining green onions and sesame seeds on top, and serve warm.
